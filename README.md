# Chess App

Play chess against Stockfish in your browser.

- 10 computer levels, 400–2600 Elo
- Your session Elo starts at 800 and updates after each finished game
- Chess.com-style move ratings: Brilliant, Best, Excellent, Good, Book, Inaccuracy, Mistake, Blunder, with live accuracy
- Evaluation bar, clocks, hints, take-backs, and promotion choice
- Chess.com-style look: dark background, green/cream board by default, with Green, Brown, Blue, Slate, and Wood board themes. Pieces are locally drawn SVG recreations in the Neo style; no Chess.com files are included.

Stockfish 10 runs entirely in your browser. Open `index.html` to play, or deploy this folder as a static site (for example, on Netlify).
